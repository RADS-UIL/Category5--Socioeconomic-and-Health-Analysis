## 1. CPR and mCPR at District Level, 2024

> Location: Pakistan · Year: 2024

Two district-level maps of Pakistan showing the contraceptive prevalence rate (CPR) and the modern contraceptive prevalence rate (mCPR), with a Karachi inset to show its districts clearly. Districts without data (Gilgit-Baltistan and parts of AJK) are shown in grey.

**Key results**

- CPR ranges from 10-15% in much of Balochistan to 45-50% in northern Punjab (Rawalpindi, Chakwal, Jhelum, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Narowal, Lahore).
- mCPR is lowest in Dera Bugti and Nasirabad (5-7.5%) and highest in a few Punjab districts and Karachi East/Central (30-35%).
- Sindh sits mostly in the middle of the range, and KP is mixed, with the former tribal districts lowest for mCPR.

## 2. District Human Development Index (HDI)

> Location: Pakistan

Four district-level maps of Pakistan: a health index (life expectancy at birth), an income index (GDI per capita), an education index (mean and expected years of schooling), and the combined HDI map that brings the three together. They show where human development is strongest and weakest across districts.

**Key results**

- Life expectancy ranges from 60-62 years (Dera Bugti, Nasirabad, Rajanpur, North Waziristan, Kech) to 71-73 years (Chitral and central Punjab).
- Income per capita is grouped into five classes (452-807 up to over 1,520), with the highest classes clustered in Punjab, Quetta and Karachi.
- The education map plots mean schooling (0.9-9.4 years) against expected schooling (1.4-6.7 years). Central and northern Punjab are strongest, and southern Sindh and parts of Balochistan are weakest.
- The combined HDI map is highest across Punjab and lowest in Sindh and Balochistan.

## 3. CLMIS Contraceptive Stock, Consumption and Outreach Maps

> Location: Pakistan (outreach map: Sindh) · Year: 2022

Static district maps built from the Contraceptive Logistics Management Information System (CLMIS). They cover condom stock per 100,000 married women of reproductive age (MWRA), consumption as a share of available stock, and a Sindh bivariate map of outreach per facility against Penta-3 dropout rate by month.

**Key results**

- Stock is very uneven: many Balochistan districts are in the lowest band (0-35,719 per 100,000 MWRA), while much of Punjab is in the top band (over 340,807).
- Consumption is below half of available stock in most of Punjab and parts of Sindh, which suggests overstocking or low uptake. Kech, Sibi, Kambar and Karachi sit in the 0-25% band.
- The monthly Sindh maps show which districts combine high Penta-3 dropout with low outreach, and how that changes from January to December.
